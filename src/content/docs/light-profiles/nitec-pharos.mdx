## Overview
The Nitec Pharos is a solid profile light - it has zoom adjustment, 4xFraming Shutters, a gobo slot and a gel frame.

The gobo accepts M Size Gobos (66mm outer/48mm inner)

## Maintenance Notes
* Deep cleaning the lenses requires a hex key to remove the front of the light so you can slide the two lenses out to clean. It's difficult to get the plastic front back on. Good luck.
